# Water Drainage Domain

## Purpose
This document details the Water Drainage Domain plugin (Status: Proposed).

## Content
### Domain Specifications
- **Classes**: `Pipe Leakage`, `Drainage Blockage`, `Open Sewer`.
- **Sensors**: Flowmeters, pressure sensors (future scope).
- **Optimization**: Prioritize leaks near residential drinking water networks.
