# Flood Risk Domain

## Purpose
This document outlines the Flood Risk Domain plugin.

## Content
### Domain Specifications
- **Inputs**: Rainfall patterns, local elevation contours, river levels.
- **Outputs**: Spatial flood risk maps.
- **Mitigation Planning**: Route emergency pumps to low-lying areas.
