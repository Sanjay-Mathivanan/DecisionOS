# Road Damage Domain

## Purpose
This document outlines the Road Damage Domain plugin.

## Content
### Domain Specifications
- **Classes**: `Pothole`, `Alligator Crack`, `Rutting`, `Manhole Defect`.
- **CV Architecture**: YOLOv8 model fine-tuned on the Global Road Damage Detection (GRDDC) dataset.
- **Optimization Inputs**: Cost of asphalt, team travel distances, patching speed.
