# Research Questions

## Purpose
This document lists the specific research questions answered by this project.

## Content
### Core Research Questions

#### RQ1 (Multimodal Severity Prediction)
> **Can multimodal information improve civic-incident severity prediction compared with text-only analysis?**
- *Baseline*: Text Classifier (BERT).
- *Proposed*: Combined Text + Vision + GIS + History.

#### RQ2 (Duplicate Detection)
> **Can multimodal similarity metrics improve duplicate civic-incident detection?**
- *Compare*: Text similarity vs. Text + GPS vs. Text + GPS + Image.

#### RQ3 (Risk Forecasting)
> **Can predictive hotspot modeling provide earlier warnings than reactive complaint handling?**
- *Measure*: Forecasting accuracy (MAE, RMSE), lead time, and false alarm rate.

#### RQ4 (Constraint Optimization)
> **Can optimization algorithms reduce response times compared with greedy/manual allocations?**
- *Measure*: Response times, travel costs, and resource utilization.
