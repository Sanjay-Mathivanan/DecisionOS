# Research Overview

## Purpose
This document frames the academic research objectives of the DecisionOS Civic platform.

## Content
### Focus
Rather than functioning as a standard software development project, this work investigates several key challenges in geospatial machine learning and constraint optimization.

### Research Themes
1. **Multimodal Fusion**: Comparing single-channel models with integrated text-vision models.
2. **Operations Research**: Evaluating scheduling models against baseline dispatches.
