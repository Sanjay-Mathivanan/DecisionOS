# Problem Statement

## Purpose
This document presents the full problem statement and background context for the DecisionOS Civic platform.

## Content
### Background
Modern municipal administrations receive vast streams of information regarding damaged roads, drainage blockages, broken streetlights, environmental hazards, and water leaks. However, traditional systems are engineered solely as digital registration forms. They register a ticket, assign it to a department, and track its status.

### The Core Challenge
The central problem is not a lack of citizen reporting, but rather:
> **How can heterogeneous, fragmented civic data be transformed into reliable, predictive, and explainable decisions that help authorities proactively manage services and efficiently allocate highly constrained resources?**

### Example Scenario
- 50 citizens write different complaints about a "large pothole near the school."
- A traditional system registers 50 independent tickets, assigning them to different workers or closing them individually.
- This creates administrative overhead, fails to recognize the incident's unified nature, and does not analyze its recurrence pattern.
